Free Buyer's Guide

Buying Your Home, Step by Step

Free, 8 pages. I'll email it to you myself, usually within a few hours.

You'll also get my monthly newsletter, Front Porch Notes. You can stop it anytime, and I never share your email.

What's inside

Thinking about buying? This short guide walks you through it in order, from getting pre-qualified to the day you get the keys.

Part 1: Getting Ready to Buy

Getting pre-qualified, then pre-approved. Choosing a lender, and the costs to plan for besides the down payment. The buyer agreement, my fee, and your wish list.

Part 2: Finding Your Home

Getting new listings by email, sending me the homes you like, keeping notes as we tour, and what to look at on a showing.

Part 3: From Offer to Closing

Deciding what to offer, the seller's disclosure form, earnest money, due diligence, inspections and repairs, the appraisal, why to hold off on new credit, and closing day.

Free guide for owners selling on their own

Selling Your Home on Your Own, Step by Step

Free, 8 pages, plus the South Carolina disclosure form. I'll email it to you myself, usually within a few hours.

You'll also get my monthly newsletter, Front Porch Notes. You can stop it anytime, and I never share your email.

What's inside

Selling your home yourself? That's your call, and I respect it. This 8-page guide walks you through it in order: pricing, showings, and closing in South Carolina. It's honest about the parts that usually trip owners up.

It comes with the South Carolina disclosure form and a one-page showing checklist.

Part 1: Pricing It Right on Your Own

Where to find real sold prices, why online estimates miss, why starting high costs you, and what selling on your own still costs.

Part 2: Getting Buyers in the Door

Where buyers actually look, tips for better photos, showings and safety, the yard sign, and what to do when agents call.

Part 3: Offers, Paperwork, and Closing in South Carolina

What you must tell buyers by law, the contract, the due diligence period, repair requests, the appraisal, the closing attorney, and a checklist from offer to closing.

What's inside

Thinking about selling? This short guide walks you through the whole sale, from getting ready to list to the day you hand over the keys. It's 8 pages, in plain English, and written by me from my own sales here in the Upstate.

Part 1: Getting ready to list

What to fix, what to clean, and what can wait. The papers to gather. How to get ready for photo day. How we set your price. And how to buy your next home at the same time.

Part 2: While your home is for sale

How buyers find your home. How showings get booked, how the lockbox works, and what to do before buyers arrive. And what to make of buyer feedback.

Part 3: From offer to closing

What happens when an offer comes in. The due diligence period, repair requests, the termite inspection, and the appraisal. A week-by-week look at the road to closing, and what to expect on closing day.
